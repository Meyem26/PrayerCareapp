## Full description (App Store + Play)

```
PrayerCare helps Christians, prayer groups, ministries, and churches organize prayers, remember to pray, care for people intentionally, and celebrate answered prayers—all in one peaceful place.

HAVE YOU EVER SAID “I’LL PRAY FOR YOU…” AND FORGOTTEN?
PrayerCare was built for those moments. Capture a burden, turn it into prayer, root it in Scripture, return day by day, and walk with others so no one prays alone.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Personal prayer journal with schedules (daily, weekly, until answered)
• Today’s prayer list so you know what to lift up
• AI prayer assistant (optional) to help shape Christ-centered words
• Scripture suggestions and sermon notes
• Prayer groups for families, small groups, and ministries
• Care actions — visits, meals, calls, follow-up
• Praise reports when God answers

PrayerCare is currently in private beta. Join the waitlist at www.prayercare.online, then create your account with the same email.
```
